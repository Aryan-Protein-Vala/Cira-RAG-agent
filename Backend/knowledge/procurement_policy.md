# Procurement & Purchasing Policy (v3.1)

Owner: Head of Procurement. Effective 01 January 2025.

## Approval thresholds

- Up to INR 100,000: department manager approval on the purchase request (OPRQ).
- INR 100,001 – INR 1,000,000: department head plus Procurement Manager.
- Above INR 1,000,000: CFO approval and a minimum of three comparative quotations.
- Capital expenditure of any value requires a signed CAPEX form referenced in the PO remarks.

## Vendor management

- New vendors must complete due diligence before a Business Partner master record (OCRD, CardType = S) is created.
- Vendor payment terms default to 45 days from invoice date unless negotiated centrally.
- Single-source purchases above INR 500,000 require a documented justification.
- Vendor performance is reviewed quarterly on delivery reliability, quality rejects and price variance.

## Purchase orders in SAP Business One

- Every goods purchase must be raised as a Purchase Order (OPOR) before the goods are received.
- Goods Receipt PO (OPDN) must be posted within 48 hours of physical receipt.
- A/P invoices (OPCH) are matched three-way: PO, goods receipt and invoice. Variances above 2% are blocked.
- Purchase orders open for more than 90 days past their due date are reported to the Procurement Manager weekly.
