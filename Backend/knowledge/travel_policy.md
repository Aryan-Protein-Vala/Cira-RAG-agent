# Travel & Expense Policy (v2.3)

Effective 01 April 2024. Owner: Finance Shared Services. Applies to all employees and contractors.

## Booking rules

- Domestic flights must be booked at least 14 calendar days in advance; international flights 21 days in advance.
- All bookings go through the approved travel desk in SAP Business One (Purchase Request → travel category).
- Business Class requires written manager approval when the net fare exceeds USD 3,000.
- Rail travel is preferred for journeys under 400 km where the total travel time is within two hours of flying.

## Accommodation limits

- Tier 1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Singapore, Dubai, Frankfurt, Chicago): USD 250 per night.
- Tier 2 cities: USD 160 per night.
- Long stays above 14 nights must use serviced apartments and require Finance approval.

## Per diem

- Domestic per diem: INR 2,500 per day, inclusive of meals and local transport.
- International per diem: USD 75 per day, or USD 95 in Tier 1 cities.
- Per diem is not payable when meals are provided by the customer or at a company event.

## Expense claims

- Expense reports must be submitted within 5 business days of returning.
- Receipts are mandatory for every line above INR 500 / USD 10.
- Claims older than 45 days are rejected automatically by the workflow.
- Reimbursement runs twice monthly, on the 15th and the last working day.
